About MedSpir AI

Built by someone who has stood on both sides of the prescription pad.

MedSpir AI is founder-led, advisor-backed, and shaped by direct exposure to pharmacy practice and computational drug discovery.

Founder

Dr. Tamara Kavithe Kimeu

Founder & CEO, MedSpir AI

Tamara founded MedSpir AI to close a gap she saw directly: pharmacovigilance and MDR-TB treatment decisions in African clinical settings often lack tooling built for their specific data and treatment realities.

She is a pharmacy student at United States International University – Africa (USIU-Africa) and a graduate of the EMT-CADDA Fellowship (Cohort 1, 2025–2026) in computational drug discovery, bringing both clinical training and computational method into MedSpir's product decisions.

Mission

Make patient safety computable, explainable, and local to African health systems.

We build systems that clinicians can trust because they can see the evidence behind every flag — not black-box scores. Every claim we make about our own product follows the same rule: no fabricated metrics, no overstated partnerships, and explicit flags on what's still missing.

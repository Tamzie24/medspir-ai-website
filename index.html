Why now

Africa carries a disproportionate MDR-TB and ADR burden — with the least tooling to catch it early.

Adverse drug reactions and drug-resistant tuberculosis are two of the clearest places where earlier signal detection changes outcomes. Most existing pharmacovigilance tooling wasn't built for the data environments or treatment protocols common across African health systems.

MedSpir AI is designed from the ground up for that context — explainable by default, and built in step with pharmacists and clinicians rather than after the fact.

The platform

One clinical intelligence layer, two purpose-built modules.

Each module is designed to be explainable end-to-end: every output traces back to the evidence that produced it.

Module 01

ADR Detection & Pharmacovigilance

A modular pipeline that identifies potential adverse drug reactions from clinical data and produces an auditable trail for every flag it raises — built for clinician trust, not just model accuracy.

01

Extractor

Pulls structured signal from raw clinical records.

02

Normalizer

Standardizes drugs, doses, and terminology.

03

Interaction engine

Checks known and inferred drug interactions.

04

Lab engine

Correlates lab values against expected ranges.

05

Syndrome engine

Matches symptom clusters to known ADR syndromes.

06

Scoring engine

Produces an explainable, evidence-linked ADR score.

Module 02

MDR-TB Treatment Optimization & Drug Repurposing

Decision support for one of the hardest problems in African clinical care: constructing effective regimens for multi-drug-resistant tuberculosis when standard protocols fail.

Drug combination prediction

AI-driven prediction of effective MDR-TB drug combinations, grounded in pharmacological interaction data.

Repurposing signals

Surfaces existing compounds with repurposing potential for MDR-TB regimens, narrowing the search space for clinicians and researchers.

Design principles

What makes the platform explainable.

Evidence-linked

Modular architecture

Each engine can be inspected, tested, and improved independently.

Clinician-reviewed
